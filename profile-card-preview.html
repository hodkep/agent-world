Behaviour

  • Click an agent: the chat opens on the right and the card opens, as you described. It is bound to the selected agent and follows it.
  • Where it opens: on the side of the stage away from the agent you clicked, so it never covers them. Click someone on the lounge sofa and the card opens over the work room; click someone at a desk and it opens over the lounge.
  • Space: it is a page overlay, not part of the canvas, so it does not zoom or pan. Wide window: full card. Medium: a compact card (face and four numbers). Narrow, or too little height to fit without covering the office: it folds into the panel above the chat, with a Show / Hide button. The Window buttons above let you try each.
  • Body: waist-up, not just the head. The head turns through five frames, the body turns about half as far, the chest rises a pixel with each breath, and the hands do what the agent does: typing while it works, holding a steaming mug when idle, arms folded and a bowed head when asleep.
  • Accessibility: the card is decorative. Every fact is also in the side panel (shown as text), and all actions stay in the panel, as the product rules require.
  • Reduced motion: the figure stays on the front frame; nothing turns, blinks or steams.
  • Cost: a small canvas that only runs while a card is showing; it pauses when the window is hidden.
